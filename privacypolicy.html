Privacy

Gym Tracker keeps your data on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Health data (steps)

With your permission, the Android app reads today's step count from Health Connect to show it on the Steps card and count toward your goal. It only reads steps, never writes anything to Health Connect, and the step numbers are stored only on your phone. You can remove the permission at any time in Health Connect settings.

What stays on your phone

Workouts, meals, water, steps, supplements, body measurements, gym fees and settings are saved only in the app's storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them, so export a backup from More → Backup now and then.

Friends

If you open Friends, the app shares a small status under your random friend code: your name, your streak (days in a row with a workout or rest day) and, only while “I lead workouts” is on, the list of exercises you added today. It goes through the free relay ntfy.sh and expires after 12 hours. Anyone who has your code can read it. Nothing else is shared. If you never open Friends, nothing is shared.

Notifications

Reminders for water, supplements, gym fees and rest timers are scheduled on your phone. They don't involve any server.
